### Buyer Personalization & Recommendation System

I want to implement a personalized recommendation system for buyers.

#### Buyer Onboarding Survey

After a user selects **Buyer** or **Buyer & Seller** during onboarding, present an optional preference survey.

The survey should contain approximately 5 questions designed to understand the buyer's interests and help personalize their experience.

Example questions:

1. Which product categories are you most interested in?

   * Electronics
   * Fashion
   * Books
   * Housing & Rentals
   * Events & Tickets
   * Services
   * Food & Delivery
   * Other

2. Which services are you most likely to use?

   * Tutoring
   * Graphic Design
   * Programming
   * Transport
   * Photography
   * Cleaning
   * Other

3. What is your typical shopping budget?

   * Low Budget
   * Medium Budget
   * High Budget

4. How often do you shop on marketplaces?

   * Daily
   * Weekly
   * Monthly
   * Occasionally

5. What are you currently looking for?

   * Products
   * Services
   * Housing
   * Events
   * Multiple Categories

All survey questions are optional. Users may skip the survey entirely and continue using the platform.

---

### Recommendation Algorithm

The recommendation engine should not rely only on the survey.

The system should continuously learn from user behavior, including:

* Search history
* Product views
* Service views
* Listing clicks
* Wishlist additions
* Cart additions
* Purchases
* Seller follows
* Likes and reactions
* Shares
* Time spent viewing listings
* Categories frequently visited

The survey should only provide initial recommendation signals for new users (cold start problem).

As users interact with the platform, behavioral data should gradually become more important than survey responses.

---

### Recommendation Scoring

Create an interest scoring system where each category accumulates points based on user actions.

Example:

* Survey category selected = +10 points
* Search in category = +5 points
* Listing click = +3 points
* Wishlist add = +8 points
* Cart add = +10 points
* Purchase = +20 points
* Follow seller = +10 points

The system should continuously update these scores and use them to rank content shown on:

* Home page
* Recommended products
* Recommended services
* Suggested sellers
* Personalized notifications
* Promotional campaigns
